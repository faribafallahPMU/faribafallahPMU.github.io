About me

After your session

Aftercare

Your care makes the difference. Tap a treatment to see the steps.

Brow aftercare
  • First 2 daysDon't wash or shower your brows.
  • For 5 daysApply the aftercare cream I give you once a day.
  • For 1 week, please avoidSauna, swimming, sun tanning or direct sun, and facial treatments.
  • After around 2 to 3 weeksCome back for your touch-up session.
Lip blush aftercare
  • If you smokeAvoid smoking for at least 3 hours after the procedure. When you smoke, avoid pressing your lips together, to protect the pigment.
  • Drinks on the first dayDuring the first 24 hours, please drink only with a straw.
  • Hot food and drinksAvoid them for the first 24 hours.
  • History of cold soresPlease start taking cold sore medication before the procedure or on the same day.
  • Aftercare creamApply the cream I give you whenever your lips feel dry. Depending on your skin, your lips may become dry or slightly flaky while healing; if they feel very dry, you may also use a colourless lip balm.
Brow lamination aftercare
  • After each washWhile your brows are still damp, comb them into shape and let them dry, so they set in place.
  • How long it lastsAbout 2 months.
Lash line aftercare
  • First 2 daysDon't wash or shower your eyes.
  • Hands offPlease avoid touching or rubbing your eyes.
  • For 4 daysAvoid mascara and any eye makeup.
