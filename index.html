About

Beauty, refined.

Hello, I'm Fariba, a permanent makeup artist in Dubai Media City. I help women feel confident in their own face with treatments that enhance what is already there rather than hide it.

I specialise in microblading, powder brows, lip blush and the cover-up of old tattooed brows. I work one-on-one in a calm, private studio, and every result is drawn on and approved by you before we start.

  • Single-use needles and strict hygiene
  • Consultation and pre-drawing before every treatment
  • Natural results, with full aftercare guidance
